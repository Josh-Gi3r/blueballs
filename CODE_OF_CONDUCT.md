# Community Code of Conduct

Blueballs welcomes contributors who want to improve open financial
infrastructure. Participation requires professional, respectful collaboration.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss code, evidence and outcomes rather than attacking people.
- Make technical disagreement specific, reproducible and open to correction.
- Welcome contributors across experience levels, identities and backgrounds.
- Protect private security reports, credentials and customer information.
- Declare relevant commercial interests when discussing provider integrations.
- Respect review boundaries, licensing and attribution.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised attention, sustained disruption,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or vulnerabilities, and retaliation
against good-faith reporters are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, lock
threads or ban participants when conduct threatens the community or project.
Responses should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid unnecessary
disclosure about reporters or affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile or another private maintainer channel. Security vulnerabilities must use
the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not a conduct report or
public issue.

Maintainers should acknowledge a report, preserve evidence, avoid conflicts of
interest and tell the reporter the outcome when it is safe to do so. This policy
applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, project chat and public
events where someone represents Blueballs.
